### Full description (max 4000 chars)
```
Boxing Interval Timer is a fast, no-nonsense round timer for boxing and combat
sports — plus interval (HIIT) training, a stopwatch, and workout tracking. No
ads, no account, fully offline.

FIGHT TIMER
• Ready-made presets: Boxing, MMA, K1, Muay Thai, BJJ, Judo, Wrestling, Taekwondo
• Adjust warm-up, rounds, round time, and rest on the fly — or save your own custom profiles
• Big, glanceable timer with a background that follows the phase: green during a round, red during rest
• Round bell and a 10-second warning; works even in silent mode and ducks (doesn't stop) your music
• Vibration on every round change, and the screen stays awake while you train

INTERVAL TRAINING
• Work/rest HIIT timers for outdoor running, treadmill, air bike, and bag work
• Beginner, intermediate, and advanced levels

MORE
• Stopwatch with laps
• Workout history and statistics: streaks, total training time, weekly progress, and a workout heatmap
• Todo list to plan your sessions

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No accounts, no servers, no trackers, no ads. Everything stays on your device.

Optional tip jar to support development — completely voluntary.
```

## In-app purchases
- Yes — a voluntary tip jar. Three consumable products (to create under Monetize → Products → In-app products), matching the iOS product intent:
  - `box.tip.small`  — small tip
  - `box.tip.medium` — medium tip
  - `box.tip.large`  — large tip
  (Product IDs must be created in the Play Console; prices set per market. The
  in_app_purchase plugin reads them by product ID at runtime.)
